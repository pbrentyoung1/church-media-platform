# User Personas – Church Media Platform

## 1. Pastor Steve – The Vision Caster
- **Role:** Lead Pastor
- **Goals:** Wants to get the church’s message to a broader audience through Roku and streaming apps. Seeks simplicity and trust in the tools.
- **Pain Points:** Tech overwhelm, lack of volunteers, time constraints.
- **Behaviors:** Delegates most of the setup to staff or volunteers but wants visibility and control over content.
- **Technical Proficiency:** Low to medium.
- **Touchpoints:** Dashboard overview, content scheduling, impact reports.

---

## 2. Maria – The Volunteer Media Director
- **Role:** Manages weekend slides, livestreams, and uploads.
- **Goals:** A streamlined system with minimal friction between Sunday recordings and Monday uploads.
- **Pain Points:** Juggling multiple platforms, inconsistent tools, lack of support.
- **Behaviors:** Tech-savvy, fast learner, thrives with clear documentation.
- **Technical Proficiency:** High.
- **Touchpoints:** Upload interface, media library, encoder integration, training wizard.

---

## 3. Tyrone – The Worship Pastor
- **Role:** Curates worship sets and post-service engagement media.
- **Goals:** Ensure worship experiences look professional across platforms.
- **Pain Points:** Low-quality uploads, syncing issues between Vimeo and Resi.
- **Behaviors:** Creative, quality-focused, occasionally frustrated with tech.
- **Technical Proficiency:** Medium to high.
- **Touchpoints:** Branding settings, media previews, Resi/Vimeo integrations.

---

## 4. Jessica – The Admin Assistant
- **Role:** Manages emails, bulletins, events, and often uploads sermons.
- **Goals:** Needs repeatable tasks and step-by-step wizards.
- **Pain Points:** Limited training time, high multitasking demand.
- **Behaviors:** Relies heavily on built-in help and automations.
- **Technical Proficiency:** Medium.
- **Touchpoints:** Onboarding, weekly upload wizard, reminder emails.

---

## 5. Brian – The End User
- **Role:** Church member who watches sermons on Roku.
- **Goals:** Wants easy access to past sermons or live streams.
- **Pain Points:** Confusing menus, inconsistent UI, video not loading.
- **Behaviors:** Non-technical, uses Roku like cable.
- **Technical Proficiency:** Low.
- **Touchpoints:** Roku app UI, search/discover interface, playback quality.
